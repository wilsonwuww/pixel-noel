# christmas-art-direction

Use straight-on front perspective, cold blue snow outside, warm amber wood and stone inside. The layered natural fir is the dominant silhouette. Inspect DESIGN.md and current screenshots. Keep movable props out of architectural backgrounds. Never use a full reference image as an editable scene. No emoji assets, triangle trees or arbitrary furniture rotation. Disclose missing reference images. Review actual composited screenshots before accepting art.
